## Editorial limits to preserve

- The three purposes (knowing Allah, being tested through deeds, and worshipping Him) are presented as the lecturer's pedagogical grouping of the cited verses, not as an exhaustive creed formula.
- The lecture's explanation of the words *Rabb* and *Ilah* is presented as the speaker's explanation; it is not silently upgraded to a universal linguistic or scholarly consensus.
- The speaker's statement about the Prophet's degree of knowledge/fear is not reproduced as a verbatim hadith because its exact source/reference was not identified in the audio notes.
- The description of deeds as sincere and in accordance with revelation is attributed to the lecturer's explanation; the exact supporting citation used in this recording was not identified.
- Avoid adding details about the names/appearance of the angels or other grave matters beyond the short cited excerpt.
